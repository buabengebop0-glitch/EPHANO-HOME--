About EPHANO

More Than Maintenance.
We Take Responsibility.

EPHANO Home & Property Management provides professional property-care services designed to make owning and maintaining a property easier. We help homeowners, landlords, tenants, Airbnb owners, investors and property owners who may not always be available to personally look after their property.

Whenever your property needs attention, EPHANO can help with:

  • Property inspections
  • Cleaning
  • Maintenance
  • Minor repairs
  • Painting
  • Property-care plans
  • General property management
  • House renting assistance

Whenever you need someone reliable to take responsibility for the care of your property, EPHANO is a phone call, message or click away.

What We Do

Property Care, Handled End to End.

Seven services. One point of contact. Tell us what your property needs.

Home Inspection

Professional property inspections to identify maintenance problems, visible defects, cleaning needs, repair requirements and general property condition.

Janitorial Cleaning Services

General cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in cleaning, move-out cleaning, post-construction cleaning and property preparation.

Maintenance & Minor Repairs

Minor plumbing, minor electrical work, carpentry, fixtures, general maintenance and other minor property repairs. Specialised or dangerous work may require a qualified licensed professional, which we can help coordinate.

Painting Services

Interior painting, exterior painting, property refresh, repainting and basic surface preparation.

Property Care

Routine property visits, condition checks, maintenance monitoring, cleaning and repair coordination, property reports, and before-and-after photographs where appropriate.

House Renting Agent

Looking for a house to rent, or have a property available for rent? EPHANO provides rental assistance to help prospective tenants and property owners connect.
